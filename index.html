Software Development Engineering Intern — ML & Computer Vision

Robosys Automation

Perception and charting for an autonomous maritime navigation product: C#/.NET on an NVIDIA Jetson, feeding a real ARPA display on the bridge.

Detection

  • Trained YOLOv8 vessel-detection models in Roboflow across several public and in-house maritime datasets.
  • Wrote the Python tooling that converts, dedupes and labels tens of thousands of frames, so a new dataset becomes trainable in an afternoon instead of a week.

Navigation math

  • Turned pixel detections into true bearing and range, then into NMEA TTM target messages streamed over UDP to the ARPA.
  • Wrote an AIS decoder from scratch in C# — !AIVDM/!AIVDO six-bit unpacking, multipart reassembly, position and static reports — and mapped AIS ship-type codes onto the product’s vessel taxonomy to cross-check what the camera saw.

Sensor integration

  • Built the multi-sensor runtime that polls camera, AIS and forward-looking sonar, converts each to a common target format, and streams them out with per-source target-ID partitioning — sensors stay distinguishable on the display, per the product ICD, rather than force-fused into one track.
  • Integrated FarSounder forward-looking sonar (SonaSoft SDK): georeferenced seabed and in-water detections into the risk map, with vessel-aware risk banding, target-strength and depth filtering, and a data path that auto-switches between recorded playback and a live sonar head.
  • Led the technical integration meeting with FarSounder solo — built the deck, presented it, and drove alignment on getting their live SDK into the product.

Charts & risk

  • Designed a source-agnostic adapter layer that normalizes anything — NOAA ENC charts, S-102 bathymetry, geofence text, live feeds — into one canonical geo-polygon format drawn over real charts, with per-zone hover metadata and go/no-go risk tiers for the route planner.
  • Pulled in live, license-free public data: NOAA/NWS marine warnings and forecast zones, and RI DEM/RIGIS restricted areas over ArcGIS REST and GeoJSON.
  • Shipped the WinForms chart app to a shared repo for team handoff.

Data collection

  • Built a WPF capture application for gathering training imagery: four camera sources (SDI frame-grabber, device, screen), UDP/serial NMEA GPS with nearest-in-time frame-to-fix syncing, per-frame metadata CSV, and a session browser for reviewing what was captured.

2026 · ongoing

RC data-collection boat

A retrofit inflatable dinghy turned into a mobile camera platform for gathering real maritime training imagery — dual electric thrusters with differential steering and a four-camera GMSL rig.

  • Specified and wired the industrial control system end to end: an Axiomatic SAE J1939 I/O controller, Deutsch DTM 12-pin and M12/NMEA 2000 connectors, 120 Ω bus termination, brought up over a USB-CAN converter.
  • Configured four 50 Hz PWM outputs to drive two vectored-steering servos (Pololu jrk G2 position controllers) and two brushed ESCs.
  • Debugged the whole bring-up solo on the bench — baud-rate and CAN-termination faults, a slipping servo feedback pot, recalibrated feedback and PID.
  • Set fail-safe neutrals: lose signal and the motors stop and the steering centers.

Firebird cooling controller

A temperature-triggered fan system for a 1984 Pontiac Firebird, taken from breadboard to a manufactured board bolted into the car.
